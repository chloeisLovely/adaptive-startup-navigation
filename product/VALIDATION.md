## Verification boundaries

- LLM success/error/malformed-output cases were **controlled proxy responses**. They prove schema enforcement and fallback behavior, not live model quality, live endpoint availability or production Worker deployment. The updated Worker was tested locally, not deployed to Cloudflare.
- Voice fallback was tested. Actual microphone capture/transcription was not tested in a headless browser and depends on browser/service support.
- WebGL, keyboard and reduced-motion execution paths were exercised. A full screen-reader or WCAG audit was not performed.
- Timed comprehension, founder willingness to pay, repeat use and real decision improvement require user studies. Earlier n=190/SUS research does not validate the redesigned v4 UX.
- The four-field starting presets are explicit, editable assumptions. The engine represents interviews through its existing segment-test rule and does not model cumulative customer counts, actual cash-flow accounting or all business models.
- No accounts, billing, cloud persistence, live market-search backend or external analytics collector was introduced. Journal persistence is on this browser; export important records.
