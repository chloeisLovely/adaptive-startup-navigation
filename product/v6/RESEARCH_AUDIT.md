Publisher/university metadata and available abstracts were reviewed. A complete new psychometric study has not been performed. Public access is not permission to reproduce items. ASNM uses its own prompts; no commercial rights to the original questionnaire wording or translation have been obtained. See [existing item map and scoring](../trusted/RESEARCH.md).

## Historical project claims

The repository contains claims of **n=190**, **SEM indirect effect .785 / CI [.679,.903]**, **15-person usability pilot / SUS 82.2**. The audited source tree contains no underlying respondent data, complete thesis or signed pilot report sufficient to verify them. Marked **verification required**, retained as historical claims in the original pages. They are not used as proof of V6 usability, decision benefit or prediction accuracy. The absence of files does not establish that the research never occurred.

Required next evidence: original thesis/report, study dates, instrument version, consent procedure, inclusion/exclusion, response dataset or auditable aggregates, SEM specification/output and SUS calculation. Any real participant data should be reviewed privately, not committed to a public repository.

## Scoring and missingness

- Original 10-answer type calculation retained. V6 additionally records actual answers, counts, primary ties and the next distinct score tier. No type is inferred from missing answers.
- Original 12-item confidence means and 6 trade-off situations retained. Missing domains have no plotted dot. A filled radar is drawn only when every domain has an answer; coverage remains in the table.
- MBTI, experience and self-described skills only contextualize advice. They never enter financial calculations.
- Journal gaps compare user expectations with **modeled** MRR changes after at least three comparable entries. Zero opening MRR yields no growth percentage. These are not real-world calibration or personality findings.

## Market data provenance

Existing `data/trends.csv` and legacy trend cards are curated/static content, not a live search feed. New market suggestions are explicitly local hypothesis templates. User source records include URL, publisher, geography, reference date and claim definition, but are not automatically verified. The existing AI Worker has no search tool; generated prose is not evidence of market size, growth or competitors.
