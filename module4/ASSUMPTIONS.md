# Module 4 — explicit research assumptions

These rules are author-set, unvalidated scenario coefficients. They are not estimated from venture outcomes. All cash amounts are nominal amounts in the state's currency.

1. **Separate extension.** The website remains static HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Module 4 uses native ES Modules and a locally vendored Babylon.js 8.26.0 bundle. No migration to Vite or framework is required. Streamlit entrypoints remain independent and unchanged.
2. **Capital unit discrepancy.** Existing `updateCapital()` and the slider's initial label say `10 = KRW 100,000,000`; the Module 3 scoring explanation calls that same number “10억”. The adapter follows the input/display (`capital × 10,000,000 KRW`) without modifying the historical scoring function. Original rubric, factors and risks are retained in provenance.
3. **Planned vs actual staffing.** `talent.length + 1 founder` initializes the team. This is an assumption, not confirmation that those hires exist. Initial burn is KRW `6,000,000 + teamSize × 4,000,000`; initial MRR is 0. CAC 140,000, retention 73, capacity `25 + teamSize × 10`, product progress 35 with a selected technology (otherwise 20). All can be edited before commitment.
4. **Founder features.** The original Module 2 type and answers are retained. Risk tolerance and market confidence default to neutral 50. Execution readiness is `clamp(30 + 8 × planned talent count + 8 × min(technology count,2))`, an unvalidated convenience rubric. Type codes are not converted into psychometric scores and do not affect revenue formulas. Existing Module 3 calibration gap remains a provenance-related founder feature.
5. **Market information.** Demand 50, annual growth 12%, uncertainty 50 are independent defaults. Competition is 45 with a persona of at least 10 characters, otherwise 65, matching the web Module 3 rule. Trend selections remain source context; they are not falsely converted into observed market measurements.
6. **Prediction units.** BARL survival is a founder belief in percent. BARL growth is expected **annual market growth**, not MRR growth. Demand was not asked in Module 3, so the prediction stays missing unless the user adds one. Zero predictions remain zero, not “missing.” The resilience score is a heuristic index: subtraction from a survival belief is labeled a scale contrast, never a probability calibration error. It is not evidence of the user's overconfidence or underconfidence.
7. **Runway.** `cash / (monthlyBurn − mrr)` measures *net* runway. If revenue covers burn and cash is positive, runway is `null` in JSON and `∞` in the UI. Cash depletion gives 0. Consequently the supplied NovaAI sample has **20 months net runway**, versus the prompt's **11.1 months gross runway** (`200000/18000`). This distinction is explicit, rather than silently mixing definitions.
8. **Cost coefficients, no exchange rates.** `u = 1` in USD scenarios; `u = 1000` in KRW scenarios. This makes preset costs plausible in each toy scenario and **is not currency conversion or an exchange-rate claim**. Data never silently switch currencies.
9. **Timing.** One atomic turn represents a month. Decisions in fixed category order → external event → operating revenues/costs → cash close → calibration. Interim negative cash is preserved until the month closes. In-month liquidity constraints are not modeled; monthly cash shortfall is reported and a zero-cash scenario ends. The MVP assumes month-end MRR is collected for that month; no receivables, taxes, debt interest, deferred revenue or inventory.
10. **Persistence/privacy.** State and full logs stay on-device in project-scoped localStorage and optional downloaded JSON. Direct transfer uses a URL fragment that is removed immediately on arrival and is not an HTTP request parameter. No research telemetry or Module 4 LLM requests occur. This feature does not use the legacy notification form or its webhook. Source company details, predictions and reflection text are included in exported JSON; review before sharing.
11. **Replay boundaries.** Model 1.0.0 uses seed + month with fixed RNG slots. Identical inputs, choices and seed reproduce identical numeric states and events. Wall-clock timestamps are metadata supplied by the caller, not RNG input. Import replays numeric history. It provides consistency validation, not a tamper-proof research audit trail or proof of when a decision was entered.
12. **Scope.** Single founder, single browser, one live session per project, at most 60 months; no real venture synchronization, multiplayer, avatars or validated financial prediction. Scene uses an orbit/pan camera with keyboard and accessible room buttons. Outcomes are longitudinal scenario trajectories, not causal estimates for individual decisions.
