RESEARCH & VALIDATION
Preliminary evidence supports the proposed adoption pathway, tested with structural equation modeling in a Korean sample (n=190). Cognitive-bias awareness (CBA) drives perceived usefulness (PU), which drives adoption intention (AI) — indirect effect .785, 95% CI [.679, .903]; PU→AI β=.949, p<.001. A 15-participant pilot returned a System Usability Scale score of 82.2.

What holds up, and what doesn't yet
This study found support for adoption-relevant properties. It did not establish that decisions actually improve, and it makes no claim of predictive validity for venture survival. The Module 3 score is a transparent rule-based rubric; estimating its weights from real outcome data is the next research step.

All 190 respondents and all 15 pilot users are based in Korea. The Korean sample provides the initial reference point for cross-market comparison; whether the mechanism replicates under different funding norms and institutional structures is the open question.

Modules 1–3 run end to end today. Module 4 is the next technical development stage — not a shipped feature.

Methodology note
This score is not a statistically validated prediction of survival. It is an exploratory index — a weighted sum of the six factors below. No randomness is involved, so the same input always returns the same result. The weights are author-defined initial values informed by the venture-failure literature, not empirically estimated coefficients. They are hypotheses to be calibrated against longitudinal outcome data.

MODULE 04 · VENTURE DIGITAL TWIN

Venture Digital Twin

A data replica of founder, venture, and market. Pull a decision lever and read what moves — before committing real resources.

IN DEVELOPMENT
Target Architecture
The twin is the engine. Immersion is just the interface.
Venture Digital Twin Engine → Scenario Simulation → AI Agents → Immersive Interface
The immersive view is the twin's presentation layer. The twin itself is a data model.
State variables the twin replicates
Capital · Burn rate · MRR · CAC · Headcount · Market growth · Competitive intensity · Founder–market fit · Validation level

Decision levers you pull
· “Hire a marketing lead now?” → runway shortens / acquisition capacity rises / execution risk shifts
· “Move price from $29 to $49?” → revenue may rise / conversion may fall / break-even moves
· “Enter a second market?” → TAM expands / regulatory exposure rises / capital requirement rises

Where this actually stands
Module 3 implements this as a rule-based scenario engine today. The continuous levers and state-transition model described above are the target architecture, still in development. Turning the rubric into a calibrated model requires real outcome data — that is the open problem, and the part I'd most like to work on with researchers.
